Hello World!

Yeah, this is my first time writing and made a blog. This act mainly comes from my desire to share a thought in a WAY WAY formal manner. I love teaching people, thus, I love sharing my thought. But the problem is, I am facing a LOT of challenges when it comes to teaching people directly. So, it often makes my audiences confused right after the lecture.

I am aware about that and I actually trying to make things at least better. The main challenge is my word building (not world building). I have a problem in organizing my word in the way that people could understand easily. I often running in a circle in my own words or talking with a too fast tempo. The reason behind this is because my word comes as fast as my thought, so it came out unfiltered.

To tackle this challenge, the thing that I should do is re-organizing my thoughts before sharing it. In order to take this mission more serious, I dipped myself into this situation where I carefully cultivating my thoughts into bunch of words called a Blog. Here, I'll post something that I like, but since I am a programmer, I probably talks more about tech rather than politics or agriculture.

This will be a long journey, but since writing is also a thought sharing platform, I guess bored is the last thing I wanted to do even though the earth was collapsing.

I'm counting on you guys!
